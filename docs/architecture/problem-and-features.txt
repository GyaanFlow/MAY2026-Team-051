PROBLEM STATEMENT

Study libraries and reading rooms are increasingly popular among students seeking quiet and dedicated spaces for learning. However, many study libraries continue to rely on manual or semi-digital processes to manage memberships, seat bookings, attendance, payments, and daily operations. These traditional methods often lead to inefficiencies, operational delays, and poor resource utilization.

Students frequently face challenges such as the lack of real-time information on seat availability, difficulty in reserving study spaces, limited visibility into their bookings and membership status, and inconvenience when requesting booking extensions or resolving issues. Manual check-in procedures and the absence of a centralized platform also result in longer waiting times and reduced user satisfaction.

From an operational perspective, library staff must manually manage walk-in admissions, verify bookings, process offline payments, allocate seats, track attendance, handle no-shows, and resolve complaints. These repetitive administrative tasks increase the likelihood of human error and make it difficult to efficiently utilize available study spaces.

Additionally, library administrators often lack comprehensive insights into occupancy trends, peak usage hours, membership statistics, and revenue, limiting their ability to make informed decisions regarding resource allocation and policy improvements.

Therefore, there is a need for a centralized Study Library Management System that streamlines membership management, seat booking, attendance tracking, payment recording, and administrative operations while providing real-time information to students, supporting efficient daily operations for staff, and enabling data-driven decision-making for administrators. Such a system would improve operational efficiency, optimize space utilization, and enhance the overall study library experience for all stakeholders.
