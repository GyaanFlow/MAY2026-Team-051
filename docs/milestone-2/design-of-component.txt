DESIGN OF COMPONENTS

1. Student Component (Booking, Membership & Self-Service APIs)

• Securely log in to the study library portal and manage account credentials.
• View membership details, membership validity, and subscribed membership plan.
• Update personal profile and contact information.
• View real-time seat and study room availability before visiting the library.
• Reserve study seats or study rooms in advance.
• Modify or cancel bookings within the permitted time period.
• View current booking details and assigned study seat.
• Present a unique check-in code to library staff during check-in.
• Check out after completing the study session.
• View booking history, attendance history, and payment history from a unified dashboard.
• Raise complaints or support requests regarding library services.


2. Library Staff Component (Operations, Attendance & Payment APIs)

• Register new students and verify membership eligibility.
• View daily bookings, expected arrivals, and current occupancy.
• Verify student bookings using unique check-in codes.
• Perform student check-ins and check-outs.
• Handle same-day walk-in admissions by allocating available seats.
• Review booking extension requests and extend bookings or allocate alternate seats based on availability.
• Mark no-show students after the configured waiting period and release reserved seats.
• Record offline payments using cash, UPI, card, or other supported payment methods.
• Manage booking approvals and booking cancellations when necessary.
• View and resolve student complaints through a centralized complaint management system.
• Monitor daily attendance and seat occupancy to support smooth library operations.



3. Admin Component (Administration, Configuration & Analytics APIs)

• Create, update, and deactivate library staff accounts.
• Approve, suspend, or manage student memberships.
• Configure study library operating hours.
• Configure booking policies, booking limits, and available seating capacity.
• Create, modify, and manage membership plans.
• Publish announcements and notifications for students and staff.
• View dashboards displaying occupancy statistics, peak usage hours, memberships, attendance, and revenue.
• Monitor overall library performance through analytical reports.
• Manage system-wide configurations and operational policies.


4. Booking & Seat Allocation Component (Reservation Management APIs)

• Maintain real-time seat availability across all study areas.
• Allocate seats during advance bookings.
• Allocate seats for walk-in students.
• Handle booking modifications and cancellations.
• Process booking extension requests.
• Reassign seats when the originally assigned seat is unavailable.
• Automatically release seats after booking cancellations or no-shows.
• Prevent double-booking through availability validation.



5. Attendance Management Component (Check-in & Occupancy APIs)

• Generate unique booking check-in codes.
• Validate student check-in codes during arrival.
• Record check-in and check-out timestamps.
• Update seat occupancy status in real time.
• Identify no-shows after the configured grace period.
• Release reserved seats for reallocation.
• Maintain attendance history for every student.


6. Payment Management Component (Payment & Transaction APIs)

• Record offline membership and booking payments.
• Support multiple payment modes (Cash, UPI, Card).
• Maintain payment transaction history.
• Link payments with memberships and bookings.
• Generate payment receipts.
• Track payment records for administrative reporting.



7. Complaint & Communication Component (Support APIs)

• Allow students to submit complaints and support requests.
• Enable staff to review, update, and resolve complaints.
• Maintain complaint history and resolution status.
• Publish announcements and notifications to all users.
• Notify students about membership expiry, booking confirmations, booking updates, and important announcements.
